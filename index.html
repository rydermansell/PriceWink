FAQ

Common questions.

Can you build my whole website?

Yes. We build a new, mobile-friendly website for your business with your quote tool built in. It costs the same as the quote tool on its own: $249 setup and $99 a month, with hosting, your domain and updates included.

Do I have to pay before I see anything?

No. We build a free preview with your own photos, reviews and services first. You only sign up if you like it.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain is yours. If you ever cancel, we’ll transfer it to you.

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. It's a link that works on any phone or computer.

Can I change my prices?

Yes, anytime. Just tell us the new rates and we'll update them.

What about jobs that need an in-person look?

Offer a "service visit" option instead of a fixed price, and finalize the quote on site.

What if I price by size or measurements?

Customers can pick a size or enter square feet or linear feet, and the price is calculated from your rate. For jobs that need measuring on site, show a price range instead.

What if the customer doesn’t know what’s wrong?

They pick what they’re seeing, like a leak, a strange noise, or something that stopped working, and book a diagnostic visit at your set fee. You give the exact price on site.

I don’t have a website. Can I still use it?

Yes. Your quote page has its own link, so it works from your Google Business Profile, your Facebook page, or a text you send to customers who ask for a price.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We set everything up and make any changes for you. You just get the jobs by email and on your calendar, and you can call or text us anytime.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Every booking goes onto a Google Calendar that shows on your phone. Block off a day there and customers can’t book it.

Who owns my customer info?

You do. Every booking is saved to a spreadsheet shared with you. We never sell or share it, and if you cancel, you keep a copy.

How much does it cost?

$249 one-time setup, then $99 a month, whether it’s the quote tool on its own or a new website with the quote tool built in. Our first 3 clients get setup for $199.
